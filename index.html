Little Moments

To Chathumiiiiii,

I'm writing this little note because I'm not there to do some crazy things together. Recently, I started wondering why you are so capable of connecting with people and leaving a small, kind, and sweet imprint on their minds, even when they are complete strangers.

For a moment, I thought maybe you had some kind of enchanting charm that you brought with you when you came from heaven. But the problem is, I never really felt that charm myself, so there might be something else behind it… or maybe your charm just isn’t powerful enough to charm me ;-).

But after giving it some thought, I realized that the true essence of your charm is your sweet little smile. Even though you still don’t know how to smile for a picture-perfect photograph, you naturally share a kind little smile with the people you meet.

It is like the whip cracks in the "Asela Perahara" — the first thing that catches everyone's attention. Then comes your sweet voice, like the sound of a flute spreading across Kandy Lake. When people get to know you better, your giggles appear like the rhythms of traditional drummers in the Perahara.

Then you unlock your next stage: whimsical and adorable behaviour, just like the traditional Udarata dancers. Last but not least, the splendid elephant represents the energy and happiness you create around the people you meet.

But there are some days when we all need to restore our energy. I’m sorry that I wasn’t there to do something crazy together and help bring your energy back.

There is a saying: "It is the same rainy day that makes the umbrella seller happier and the ice cream seller sadder." You are like the ice cream seller — everyone likes and loves the ice cream seller, but rainy days can be difficult for him. Still, he keeps looking forward to better days.

So, let’s hope for better days.

You know who I am.
